What to Include in Your Booking Request

Providing detailed information about your celebration makes it easier to discuss suitable entertainment options.

  • Your preferred event date and time
  • The city where your event is taking place
  • The type of party or celebration
  • The approximate number of guests
  • Your preferred type of entertainer
  • Your estimated entertainment budget
  • Any themes or special requests
  • Whether your venue permits outside entertainment

Entertainment prices vary based on location, travel requirements, performance length, number of entertainers and the type of event. Always confirm the total price and booking conditions before committing to a performance.
